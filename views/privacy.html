Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

NYC Charter School Finder (“the site”, “we”) helps families find and keep track of New York City charter schools. This policy explains what information the site collects, why, and the choices you have. In short: you can browse without an account, we only keep what’s needed to save your shortlist, and you can delete everything at any time.

Browsing without an account

You can search, filter, view schools, compare them and download lists without signing in. We don’t ask for your name or email to do any of this.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google

Signing in is only needed to save schools. When you sign in, Google shares and we store:

  • your name, email address and profile photo link
  • a Google account identifier, so we can recognize you next time

We never see or store your Google password, and we don’t request access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar, Drive or any other Google data.

What you save

  • the schools in My Schools, the application status you choose for each, and any notes you write
  • a share-link code, only if you create a read-only share link

Your notes are private. They aren’t shown on share links or to anyone else.

Usage information

To understand which features help families, the site records a small set of anonymous events: searches and filters used (for example “Brooklyn, elementary”), schools opened, links clicked (such as Apply or Directions), and sign-in and save actions. These are tied to a random session code created in your browser, and to your account if you’re signed in. We don’t use advertising or third-party analytics trackers.

Your location

If you use Near me, your browser shares your location with the page only to sort schools by distance. It stays in your browser and is never sent to or stored by our server. If you type an address or zip code, it’s sent to NYC Planning’s public geocoder to find it on the map, and it isn’t saved. Locations are never put in page links.

Cookies and browser storage

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in and a short-lived cookie to protect sign-in from forgery. The site also stores a few preferences in your browser, such as language, map style and the random session code. We don’t use advertising cookies.

How we use information

  • to show your saved schools, statuses, notes and deadlines on any device you sign in from
  • to create your calendar file and share link when you ask for them
  • to keep the site secure (for example, limiting repeated requests)
  • to improve the site using the anonymous usage events described above

We don’t sell, rent or trade your information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Services we rely on

  • Google: sign-in (Google’s own privacy policy applies to your Google account)
  • Render: hosts the site and its database in the United States
  • OpenStreetMap and Esri: map and satellite images; your browser requests them directly, so they see standard request information such as your IP address
  • NYC Planning GeoSearch: turns addresses you type into map locations

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your account and saved schools until you delete them. You can remove any school from My Schools at any time, and you can stop a share link from working with Stop sharing.

To delete your account, sign in, open My Schools and choose Delete my account and data at the bottom of the page. This immediately and permanently erases your profile, saved schools, statuses, notes, share link and the usage events tied to your account, and signs you out on every device. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Security

The site uses HTTPS everywhere, verifies every Google sign-in, protects all changes against cross-site request forgery, and only lets you see and change your own saved schools.

Children

The site is meant for parents, guardians and other adults helping with school choice. It isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we’ll delete the account.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
