Plan your child’s school future with confidence

Choosing a school is one of the biggest decisions a family makes, and the charter school process in New York City is spread across hundreds of websites, deadlines and lotteries. NYC Charter School Finder puts every charter school campus in the five boroughs on one map, so you can find the right fit, keep your options organized and never miss a deadline.

Explore the map Go to My Schools

Discover

See all 436 charter school campuses across Manhattan, Brooklyn, the Bronx, Queens and Staten Island. Search by name, network or neighborhood, and filter by borough, grade level, entry grade, school district or zip code.

Compare

Open any school to see its grades, admissions steps, lottery preferences, key dates, contact details and costs. Put up to four schools side by side, and see how far each one is from home.

Organize

Save schools to My Schools to build a shortlist that follows you on any device. Track each application from “Researching” to “Accepted”, keep private notes from tours, and add every deadline to your calendar.

Share

Send a read-only link of your shortlist to a partner, grandparent or counselor so everyone helping with the decision is looking at the same list.

Our goal

Every family deserves a clear, calm way to think about their children’s education. This site exists to make the charter school search less overwhelming: one trustworthy list, plain-language details, and simple tools to plan ahead, so parents and caregivers can spend less time hunting for information and more time choosing what’s best for their kids.

Browsing is free and open to everyone, with no account needed. Signing in with Google is only required to save schools, and we keep just your name, email address and profile photo. Read our privacy policy for details.

Where the information comes from

School details come from the NYC Charter School Center and each school network’s own website, cross-checked against the New York State charter school directory and NYC Planning’s address geocoder. Schools change dates and programs from year to year, so always confirm details with the school before applying.

NYC Charter School Finder is an independent project. It isn’t affiliated with the NYC Department of Education, the New York State Education Department or any charter school.
